TFTD SENSORY MECHANICS IN RIMWORLD

Vanilla RimWorld oceans are flat textures. By querying tile.elevation < 0 on WorldGrid, Project Vigilance builds a 3-layer bathymetric mesh:

  • Acoustic Sonar Propagation: Confined exclusively to water hexes; continental landmasses act as sound walls.
  • Submersible Evasion: USOs dive into abyssal trenches to break radar contact; tracking requires coastal hydrophones.
  • Apocalyptic Nebula: Crimson cosmic void reflecting the Terror from the Deep atmosphere.
