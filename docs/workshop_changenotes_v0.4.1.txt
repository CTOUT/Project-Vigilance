[list]
[*] [b]Custom Visual Assets & Faction Livery Tinting:[/b]
    [list]
    [*] Replaced vanilla placeholder graphics across colonies and orbital entities with custom 128x128 RGBA32 functional sprites and neutral grayscale livery masks that tint cleanly to runtime faction colours.

[*] [b]Programmatic Thermal Integrity Engine:[/b]
    [list]
    [*] Startup post-def audit eliminates oxygen combustion on structural/precious metals (Steel, Plasteel, Gold, Silver, Uranium) and sensory infrastructure (radar domes, phased arrays, SIGINT consoles, sonar arrays).
    [*] Fixed-recipe all-metal/stone machinery, spacer boss chips, gravtech components, and archotech prosthetics are now non-flammable, while chemical batteries remain flammable (thermal runaway).
    [*] Aligned bridges and gravship hulls: vanilla wooden bridges retain 100% flammability while Odyssey steel Heavy Bridges, Ancient Heavy Bridges, and Substructures are 0% flammable; metal Gravship Hulls stay fireproof while Bioferrite hulls scale by material flammability.
    [/list]
[*] [b]Orbital Telemetry, Clutter Control & Deep-Orbit Fixes:[/b]
    [list]

    [*] Hidden tactical ground caravans and unmanned listening posts from deep Orbit view so only strategic player colonies appear as ground anchors without overlapping colony icons.
    [*] Added deep-orbit camera ray projection so DevMode world placement clicks spawn directly under your cursor even from Orbit view, alongside new Orbital Hub spawn/clear DevMode actions.
    [*] Restricted orbital station spawning to populated world coverage tiles and stratified orbital shells across LEO, MEO, and GEO bands.
