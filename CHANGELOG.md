## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Empty & Abandoned Settlement Hostile Incursion Tactical Hierarchy (`IncursionArrivalManager`, `WorldObject_Incursion`):**
  - Hostile incursions reaching an evacuated active player colony (zero conscious colonists present) or an abandoned settlement tile no longer abruptly disperse and vanish into thin air. Instead, they execute a 3-tier tactical decision hierarchy:
    - **Tier 1 (Probabilistic Caravan Tracking & Hot Pursuit):** Raiders actively scour the vacant site for signs of recent evacuation. If player caravans are within tracking radius ($\le 8$ hexes), raiders roll a tactical tracking chance based on technology level (Neolithic: 85%, Medieval: 65%, Industrial: 50%, Spacer: 45%, Archotech: 35%, Mechanoid: 30%), raid type (sapper penalty: -15%, hound/beast scent bonus: +15%), trail distance decay (-5%/hex), and active rainfall (-20%). On success, the incursion locks onto the fleeing caravan, transitions to hot pursuit (`[Pursuit]`), dynamically updates its intercept vector every 250 ticks as the caravan moves, and ambushes it upon reaching the same hex (`ExecuteHostileInterception(isAmbush: true)`).
    - **Tier 2 (Opportunistic Secondary Outpost Retargeting):** If tracking rolls fail or no caravans are in range, raiders evaluate reachable player colonies, camps, or listening outposts within striking distance ($\le 10$ hexes). If located, the incursion diverts course (`SetRetargetRoute`) and notifies the player via threat letter (`"Hostile Force Diverting"`) as it advances on the secondary target.
    - **Tier 3 (Fallback Ransack & Return Home March):** If no tracks are discovered and no secondary outposts are within range, the warband ransacks the vacant site and reverses course (`incursion.ReverseCourse`). The force physically marches across the Geoscape back to its origin base or friendly stronghold, remaining visible on sensors until safely exiting the map into home territory (`ReturnedHomeSafely`).

- **Central Contact Registry & Database Architecture (`ContactRegistry`):** Added a persistent `WorldComponent` tracking all active, engaged, retreating, and resolved planetary contacts indexed by unique `TrackingId`:
  - **Fast Query Service:** Provides global queries (`TryGetContact`, `GetContact`, `HostileContacts`, `RetreatingContacts`, `EngagedContacts`) and high-fidelity breakdown extraction (`ContactRosterBreakdown`) for human combatants, pack animals, mechanoids, health conditions, weapons, and provisions.
  - **Historical Operations Archive (`ContactArchiveRecord`):** Preserves an operations ledger for up to 150 resolved incursions (neutralized, departed, arrived at colony, or returned to base), recording departure/resolution ticks, initial vs final headcounts, member names, captives taken, and final outcome reasons.
- **Eager Deterministic Force Materialization (`WorldObject_Incursion.SetupIncursion`):** Incursions now pre-materialize their full deterministic force makeup (pawns, pack animals, mechanoids, weapons, armour, carried provisions, and merchant trade cargo) upon departure from their origin base rather than lazily on contact, placing them immediately into `WorldPawns` with GC protection (`KeepForever` and `ForcefullyKeptPawns`) and enabling continuous overland physiological simulation throughout their journey.
- **Night Owl & Schedule-Aware Caravan Rest (`Caravan_NightResting_Patch` & `ScoutHorizonSensor`):** Replaced vanilla RimWorld's hardcoded 22:00–06:00 player caravan rest lock with trait-aware (`NightOwl`) and `Pawn_TimetableTracker` (`Schedule` tab) rest and watch evaluation:
  - **Nocturnal / Night Owl Squads:** Colonists with the `NightOwl` trait on untouched default or all-`Anything` schedules automatically rest during daytime (**11:00–18:00** local time) and march through the night (**18:00–11:00**). Awake Night Owls negate the 50% nighttime darkness penalty on `ScoutHorizonSensor`, granting full 100% scout visibility through the night (while collapsing to `1.0` hex during their 11:00–18:00 daytime sleep window if no Daywalkers are present).
  - **Mixed Squad Majority Shift & 24/7 Watch:** Mixed caravans follow the majority circadian shift of conscious colonists for movement (defaulting to standard **22:00–06:00** rest on an exact 50/50 tie), while the opposite shift remains awake on camp watch—giving mixed parties uninterrupted **100% "day" visibility 24/7**.
  - **Schedule-Tab Override & Force-Marching:** Explicit `Sleep` or `Work`/`Recreation`/`Meditate` timetable assignments override default circadian windows, allowing custom caravan sleep shifts or force-marching through rest hours.
  - **Dynamic Rest Countdown (`CaravanNightRestUtility_LeftRestTicksAt_Patch`):** Accurately calculates remaining rest ticks when a player caravan camps outside standard 22:00–06:00 hours.
- **Threat-Aware Tactical Retreat Pathfinding (`IncursionTacticalRetreatUtility`):** Upgraded tactical retreat so fleeing colonists evaluate known/visible threats (hostile raiders, mechanoids, insectoids, and active turrets) across all four map borders and verify actual `PawnPath` trajectory exposure instead of blindly running to the nearest border into enemy fire.
- **Trader Loss & Trade Mission Abort Lifecycle (`WorldObject_Incursion.AbortTradeMission`, `Trigger_TraderLost`):**
  - If a trade caravan's designated merchant (e.g. Sandra) is killed or downed by third-party threats (wildlife, mechanoids, natural hazards, or crossfire), the expedition recognizes the mission cannot continue.
  - On tactical maps: `Trigger_TraderLost` transitions the caravan to an evasive exit (`LordToil_ExitMap(LocomotionUrgency.Jog)`), while surviving guards escort their pack animals and cargo toward safety.
  - On the Geoscape: The trade caravan cancels the rest of its itinerary, updates its contact classification to `"returning caravan (aborted mission)"`, and plots a retreat route back to their nearest faction stronghold or origin outpost via `IncursionOriginResolver.TryResolveRetreatRoute`.
  - Full arrival handling: returning survivors notify the player and cleanly disband into their home stronghold upon safe return.
- **Home Settlement Emergency Retreat:** Enabled retreating from besieged player settlements (`map.IsPlayerHome`) to form or join a player caravan on the Geoscape without vanishing into `WorldPawns`.
- **Humanlike Combat Break & Looting:** Restored opportunistic looting (`canSteal: true`) and casualty retreat transitions (`Trigger_FractionPawnsLost` / `LordToil_StealCover`) for humanlike hostile factions in field encounters and ambushes.

### Fixed

- **Hardcoded Settlement & Stronghold Name Removal (`IncursionRetreatManager`, `IncursionArrivalManager`, `WorldObject_Incursion`, `MapComponent_OverlandAmbush`, `SettlementUtility_Attack_Patch`):**
  - Eliminated hardcoded fallback strings (e.g. `"the colony"`, `"their stronghold"`, `"Colony territory"`, `"Hostile Warband"`, `"enemy forces"`, and `"the settlement"`) across letters, inspect panes, and tactical encounter records.
  - Dynamically queries world objects (`Find.WorldObjects.SettlementAt`, `MapParentAt`) to accurately reflect custom player colony names, multiple settlements, enemy strongholds (e.g. "Norwich Outpost" instead of generic "their stronghold"), and specific tile coordinates.
- **Save-Time Dangling Map Reference Elimination in Fleeing Raider Batches (`IncursionRetreatManager.RetreatBatch`):**
  - Resolved a save-time error where `IncursionRetreatManager.RetreatBatch` held a strong reference to `Map` via `Scribe_References.Look(ref map, "map")`. If raiders fled a temporary encounter map or `CaravansBattlefield` that was deinited before the 25-second batch timeout expired, saving the game triggered red errors from RimWorld's `DebugLoadIDsSavingErrorsChecker`.
  - Replaced `Map map` with `PlanetTile originTile` and `string settlementName`, completely eliminating dangling Map pointers and sanitizing dead or destroyed pawns prior to saving.
- **Stale WorldComponent Binding Leak Across World Loads (`ExternalFogCompatibility`, `SensoryManager`):**
  - Resolved an issue where returning to the main menu and loading a new game or different save left `ExternalFogCompatibility` bound to delegates and collections from the previous world.
  - Replaced one-shot `Initialize()` calls in `SensoryManager` with `ExternalFogCompatibility.ResetAndRescan()` during `FinalizeInit()`, ensuring fresh component discovery on each world load.
- **Destroyed Medicine Reuse in Overland Physiology Simulation (`WorldObject_Incursion.SimulatePawnsPhysiology`):**
  - Resolved an edge case where multiple wounded pawns tended in sequence could pass a destroyed medicine reference to `TendUtility.DoTend` after the first tend depleted the carried stack.
  - Added real-time destroyed/depletion validation and refreshed medicine lookups between successive tends.
- **Dead Captive Entity Spawning Guard in Stronghold Assaults (`SettlementUtility_Attack_Patch`):**
  - Added `!captive.Dead && !captive.Destroyed` guards before spawning incarcerated colonists during base assaults, preventing deceased captives from attempting to spawn as live pawns.
- **GitHub Actions Least-Privilege Permissions (`.github/workflows/*.yml`):**
  - Added explicit top-level `permissions: contents: read` across `format.yml`, `gitleaks.yml`, `ps-analyzer.yml`, and `spellcheck.yml` to prevent unintended privilege escalation from default repository tokens.
- **DRY Multi-Incursion Iteration Consolidation (`MapComponent_OverlandAmbush`, `IncursionTacticalRetreatUtility`, `Pawn_ExitMap_Patch`):**
  - Consolidated duplicate multi-incursion vs. single-source loops across `ExecuteStealthDisengage`, `ExecuteRetreat`, `CleanUpEncounterMap`, and `Pawn_ExitMap_Patch` by introducing `MapComponent_OverlandAmbush.GetActiveIncursionSummaries()`.
  - Removed over 120 lines of redundant boilerplate code while guaranteeing consistent handling across all retreat and disengage scenarios.
- **DRY Assembly Lock Rotation Script Modularisation (`scripts/lib/ModDeployHelper.ps1`):**
  - Extracted duplicated file-lock detection and rotation routines from `deploy-geoscape.ps1` and `package-release.ps1` into a shared, reusable PowerShell helper module.
- **Hot-Path Zero-Allocation Collision Loop Optimization (`WorldObject_Incursion.CheckInterceptionsAndPursuits`):**
  - Eliminated per-tick LINQ closure and list allocations in caravan interception and direct-tile collision checks, replacing them with index-based loops and lazy list initialization.

- **Dangling Deinited Map Reference in Tactical Encounter Letters (`ChoiceLetter_TacticalEncounter`):**
  - Resolved a save-time warning and future load error: `"Object with load ID Map_X is referenced (xml node name: battleMap) but is not deep-saved. This will cause errors during loading."` when saving after reforming a caravan from a tactical encounter map.
  - Root cause: When a player reformed a caravan from a `CaravansBattlefield` map, the temporary map was deinited and removed from `Find.Maps`. However, the tactical encounter letter (`ChoiceLetter_TacticalEncounter`) remaining in the letter stack/archive retained an unmanaged `Scribe_References.Look` to the deinited `battleMap`. Because deinited maps are not deep-saved, RimWorld's `DebugLoadIDsSavingErrorsChecker` flagged the dangling reference on save.
  - Pre-Save Reference Sanitization: Added pre-save validation in `ChoiceLetter_TacticalEncounter.ExposeData` to nullify references to deinited maps (`!Find.Maps.Contains(battleMap)`) and destroyed world objects before writing XML.
  - Auto-Dismissal on Map Deinitialisation: Implemented `CanShowInLetterStack` override to auto-dismiss obsolete tactical encounter and caravan reforming letters once the underlying battle map is deinited.
- **Tactical Ambush Disengagement & Battlefield Re-entry Loop (`WorldObject_Incursion`, `IncursionInterceptionUtility`, `MapComponent_OverlandAmbush`):**
  - Resolved an issue where evading an unaware overland raid or ambush by hiding until raiders exited the map immediately pulled the player's force back into a second duplicate encounter.
  - Root cause: When raiders reached the map perimeter and stepped off the map, `MapComponent_OverlandAmbush` marked the incursion disengaged and unflagged `IsEngagedInTacticalMap = false`, placing the force in retreat on the Geoscape. However, the incursion remained on the exact world tile where the tactical `CaravansBattlefield` map was still active. On the next tick, `WorldObject_Incursion.CheckInterceptionsAndPursuits` detected an active non-home map with conscious player pawns, failed to recognize the battlefield had already been engaged due to map IDs being pruned from `metCaravanIds`, and lacked retreat guards on battlefield transit. Consequently, it invoked `ExecuteBattlescapeTransit`, instantly respawning the exited raiders at the map edge to march across the map a second time.
  - Dedicated Map Encounter Tracking: Introduced persistent `metMapIds` tracking on `WorldObject_Incursion` to record visited and engaged map IDs independently from caravan IDs, preventing accidental pruning.
  - Tactical Map Re-entry Lockout: Guarded map collisions with `alreadyHandledByMap` and verified `ambushComp.Engagements`, ensuring an incursion that has engaged or transited across a map can never re-enter it.
  - Retreating & Turning-Back Ambush Suppression: Prevented retreating or turning-back hostile incursions from triggering battlefield transits or initiating surprise ambushes on caravans occupying their tile.
  - Encounter Cooldown Synchronization: Automatically applied a 15,000-tick (6 game hour) encounter cooldown to all player caravans on the battlefield upon disengagement or encounter initiation.
- **Empty Settlement Evacuated Caravan Interception & Mid-March Arrival Guards (`IncursionArrivalManager`, `WorldObject_Incursion`):**
  - Resolved an issue where hostile warbands arriving at an evacuated settlement while player colonists were formed in a caravan on the settlement hex (`0.0 hexes`) succeeded on their tracking roll but failed to pursue or engage, instead reporting that they ransacked vacant structures and reversed course.
  - Root cause: `IncursionOriginResolver.TryBuildPath(arrivalTile, targetCaravan.Tile)` explicitly returned `false` when `start == dest`. Because pathfinding failed for co-located caravans, the warband bypassed pursuit and fell through to Tier 3 ransack and reverse-course. On the subsequent tick, standing on the settlement hex re-triggered arrival logic, which lacked a destination distance check (`reachedDest`), immediately declaring `"returned home safely"` and destroying the incursion on tick 1 at the colony.
  - Direct Hex Interception Precedence: Reordered `WorldObject_Incursion.CheckInterceptionsAndPursuits` so world caravan collision checks take priority over settlement inspection. When player pawns are formed in a caravan on the same hex or gate distance (`dist < 0.25f`), raiders engage the mobile force immediately via `IncursionInterceptionUtility.ExecuteInterception(targetCaravan, incursion, isAmbush: true)`.
  - Robust Pursuit Pathfinding & Neighbour Fallback: Added direct neighbour fallback `{ currentIncursionTile, targetCaravan.Tile }` when pathfinding between adjacent tiles, ensuring overland pursuit reliably locks onto moving caravans.
  - Destination Verification on Return & Retreat: Guarded `ExecuteArrival` for turning back and retreating forces with `reachedDest`, verifying that the force has actually arrived at the terminus of its multi-tile return route before declaring safe arrival or deregistering the contact.
- **Empty Settlement Peaceful Incursion Return March & Accurate Outcome Logging (`IncursionArrivalManager`, `WorldObject_Incursion`):**
  - Resolved an issue where peaceful trade caravans and visitor groups reaching an empty/evacuated settlement displayed a message stating they "returned home", but were immediately destroyed in place and logged in `ContactRegistry` as `"Neutralized"`, causing their contacts to vanish from the Geoscape.
  - Peaceful visitors and traders finding no colonists present now physically turn back (`ReverseCourse`), remaining active on the Geoscape as visible contacts with updated inspect status (`Status: Returning home`) as they march back to their home territory.
  - Upon reaching their home base, they safely disband with outcome `"ReturnedHomeSafely"`.
  - Fixed false `"Neutralized"` outcome logging in `ContactRegistry` across all incursion arrival, visit, trade, and dispersal paths (properly logging `"TradedAtColony"`, `"VisitedColony"`, `"AssaultedColony"`, `"DispersedNoDefenders"`, and `"ReturnedHomeSafely"`).
  - Fixed an `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` risk in `WorldObject_Incursion.ReverseCourse` when reversing completed destination paths.
- **Dangling World Pawn Relation Sanitization on Save (`WorldObject_Incursion`):**
  - Resolved harmless but noisy load-time warnings: `"Could not resolve reference to object with loadID Thing_Human... curParent=RimWorld.DirectPawnRelation"` and `"Pawn [Name] has relation 'ExLover' with null pawn after loading. This means that we forgot to serialize pawns somewhere..."`.
  - Root cause: When RimWorld generates background pawns for an incursion, procedural relation generators occasionally roll non-essential relationships (e.g., ex-lovers) pointing to ephemeral world pawns that RimWorld's `WorldPawnGC` immediately discards. Because the incursion pawn is kept in `WorldPawns`, its relationship tracker saved a reference to the discarded pawn's ID, which failed to resolve upon loading before being auto-pruned by vanilla `Pawn_RelationsTracker`.
  - Added pre-save relationship sanitization (`SanitizeDanglingRelations`) in `WorldObject_Incursion.ExposeData` to clean up references to discarded or non-serialized world pawns before saving, eliminating cross-reference warnings on game load.
- **Trade Caravan Loot Persistence & Pack Animal Cargo Transportation (`WorldObject_Incursion`, `IncursionInterceptionUtility`):**
  - Resolved an issue where trade caravans in hostile or repeated encounters dropped their entire inventory of trade goods in a pile on the floor at their spawn position rather than acting as a functioning caravan.
  - Root cause: `IncursionInterceptionUtility.ExecuteHostileInterception` previously called `GenPlace.TryPlaceThing` on every item in `incursion.TradeStock`, littering the battlefield with trade goods and emptying the caravan's stock.
  - Replaced floor-dumping with bidirectional cargo lifecycle synchronization (`SyncCargoFromTradeStockToPawns` and `SyncCargoFromPawnsToTradeStock`):
    - When transitioning to a tactical encounter or battlescape transit map, `incursion.TradeStock` is loaded directly into the `pawn.inventory.innerContainer` of the caravan's carrier pack animals (dromedaries, muffalos, etc., falling back to humanoids if animals are absent).
    - Caravans physically carry their goods across the map during combat, transit, and evasive retreats. If a pack animal is killed or downed in combat, vanilla RimWorld drops that animal's cargo on the spot for looting.
    - When surviving caravan pawns exit or retreat from the map, their carried items are gathered back into `incursion.TradeStock`, preserving surviving trade cargo seamlessly across subsequent Geoscape interactions and sector encounters without cargo loss or duplication.
- **False Log Warning in Haze of War Color Uploads (`WorldDrawLayer_HazeOfWar`):**
  - Resolved periodic log warnings: `"Finalizing mesh which is already finalized. Did you forget to call Clear()?"` during caravan movement in Classic Haze of War.
  - Root cause: RimWorld's `LayerSubMesh.FinalizeMesh` warns if `finalized == true`. Because `WorldDrawLayer_HazeOfWar.ApplyDirtyColors()` updates vertex colors in-place without re-clearing static 3D geometry, `finalized` remained `true` from the prior frame.
  - Reset `subMeshes[subIdx].finalized = false;` immediately before calling `FinalizeMesh(MeshParts.Colors)`, silencing the false warning while keeping in-place GPU color uploads fast and allocation-free.
- **Trade Caravan Persistence & Trader Status Preservation Across Map Transitions (`WorldObject_Incursion`, `PawnComponentsUtility_AddAndRemoveDynamicComponents_Patch`, `IncursionTrackingHelper`):**
  - Resolved an issue where transiting merchant traders (e.g. bulk goods traders like Sandra) lost their trader status, trade bubble icon, and `TraderKindDef` upon transiting between maps or after reforming caravans on the Geoscape.
  - Root cause: vanilla RimWorld's `WorldPawns.PassToWorld` triggers `Notify_PassedToWorld` -> `Pawn_MindState.Reset()`, which resets `wantsToTradeWithColony = false`. When the pawn was spawned into the next sector, `PawnComponentsUtility.AddAndRemoveDynamicComponents` saw `wantsToTradeWithColony == false` and wiped `pawn.trader = null`, permanently destroying the pawn's `TraderKindDef`.
  - Added Harmony patches on `PawnComponentsUtility.AddAndRemoveDynamicComponents` and `WorldPawns.PassToWorld` to safeguard trader pawns of active trade incursions, ensuring `wantsToTradeWithColony = true` and `traderKind` are preserved and re-linked before and after spawn/despawn cycles.
  - Explicitly tracked `TraderPawn` and `explicitTraderKind` on `WorldObject_Incursion` with full Scribe save/load persistence so merchant roles persist indefinitely across multiple visited sectors.
- **Neutral Caravan False Alarm & Roadside Trading in Tactical Transit (`LordJob_IncursionTransit` & `IncursionInterceptionUtility`):**
  - Resolved an issue where friendly or neutral trade caravans transiting a tactical map falsely treated approaching player colonists as hostile ambushers within 18 cells, triggering panicked evasion and broadcasting `"Gunfire ahead! [Travellers] have detected danger..."` when no gunfire had occurred.
  - Neutral and friendly transiting incursions now ignore player colonists and only enter evasion if they hear actual combat gunfire, suffer damage, or spot true hostiles (enemies to their faction or aggressive manhunter animals).
  - Added situational threat messaging distinguishing between actual gunfire sounds heard and visual hostiles spotted.
  - Enabled roadside trading during peaceful map crossings by setting `wantsToTradeWithColony = true` on spawned merchant traders in `ExecuteBattlescapeTransit`.
- **Asymmetric Fog-of-War Hex Uncovering & Corner Clipping:** Switched fog exploration, active vision perimeters, and sensor ranges from continuous spherical geodesic distance (`ApproxDistanceInTiles`) to discrete **Topological Hex Steps** (`FloodFillTopological` & `TraversalDistanceBetween`):
  - Fixed settlement and caravan vision boundaries clipping corner tiles (e.g. bottom-left and top-right) and protruding teeth (upper-left) caused by RimWorld's Goldberg icosahedron distortion.
  - Guaranteed 100% geometric symmetry: outer active vision boundaries and fog reveals now form a clean, regular hexagonal polygon with identical hex step lengths in all directions (_Civilization_ style).
  - Aligned contact detection and optical identification thresholds directly with the visible outer cyan and inner orange boundary rings.
- **Geoscape Fog-of-War Movement Lag & Mesh Re-Triangulation Hitching:** Completely eliminated the 100–250ms freeze when moving caravans uncover new tiles in Classic Haze of War mode:
  - **Dynamic In-Place Vertex Colors:** Replaced full-globe mesh re-triangulation in `WorldDrawLayer_HazeOfWar` with pre-computed static 3D hex geometry and in-place vertex color modification (`LayerSubMesh.FinalizeMesh(MeshParts.Colors)`), dropping frame latency per reveal from ~200ms to < 0.1ms (~2,000× speedup).
  - **Selective World Layer Invalidation:** Eliminated global re-render churn by replacing blind `SetDirty()` calls on `WorldObjects_Expandable`, `WorldObjects_NonExpandable`, and `WorldDrawLayer_Landmarks` with targeted discovery checks that only invalidate layers when an actual shrouded object, settlement, or landmark feature center is uncovered.
  - **Time-Sliced Geometry Initialization:** Time-sliced the initial globe mesh construction across an incremental coroutine yielding every 3ms (`Stopwatch`), preventing game startup or world generation freezes.
  - **High-Frequency 5-Tick Caravan Movement Polling:** Added 5-tick caravan coordinate sampling in `SensoryManager` so fog uncovers smoothly and instantaneously as a caravan crosses hex borders without waiting for the 250-tick pulse.
- Fixed concealed overland ambushes immediately alerting enemy columns or routing their transit path through the player's deployment zone.
- Fixed multi-caravan tactical encounters throwing `Tried to add pawn to lord ... already a member of lord` errors when secondary transiting incursions shared a battle map.
- Fixed `Lord owns a free world pawn` error when despawning hostile raiders during a player settlement evacuation.
- Fixed formerly-neutral trade caravans that turned hostile en route displaying a false "found no defenders and dispersed" message upon arriving to assault a player settlement.

- **Multi-Vector Compass Pincers:** Enforced distinct compass approach quadrants for multi-column assaults to execute coordinated pincer strikes around the settlement.
- **Calm Tactical Amber Pulse:** Replaced high-frequency 5Hz triangle strobe with a smooth 2.5s sinusoidal breathing beacon for Unidentified Contacts across both world mesh rendering and expanding icons.
- **Native Planetary Haze of War Mesh (`WorldDrawLayer_HazeOfWar`):** Implemented native 3D mesh overlay on the RimWorld 1.7 multi-layer planetary grid (`PlanetLayer.WorldDrawLayers`) for Classic Haze mode. Triangulates sector fan geometry over altitude `0.042f` with `WorldMaterials.VertexColorTransparent`, drawing 100% opaque obsidian shroud over unexplored sectors, translucent slate-grey haze (~55% opacity) over unmonitored sectors, and crystal-clear cutouts for active sensory perimeters. Features zero-allocation submesh reuse and instant single-pass mesh finalisation.
- **WorldObject Fog Occlusion & Information Security:** Added Harmony patches (`WorldObject_Visibility_Patch`) across `ExpandableWorldObjectsUtility.HiddenByRules`, `WorldObject.SelectableNow`, `WorldObjectSelectionUtility.VisibleToCameraNow`, and `GenWorldUI.WorldObjectsUnderMouse`. Non-player outposts, cities, settlements, quest sites, and ruins in unexplored shrouded sectors have their 2D expanding icons hidden, mouse hover tooltips suppressed, and selection blocked. Explored static settlements remain visible through the translucent haze veil.
- **Instant World Load & Zero-Flicker Initialization:** Eagerly initialized the exploration bitmask and executed an initial sensory sweep during `SensoryManager.FinalizeInit(bool fromLoad)` before the globe renders. Defaulted unallocated bitset queries to shrouded (`false`), eliminating the initial flash where all sectors briefly appeared grey before turning black.

Bi-directionally reads external revealed tile state and propagates scout/radar reveals into external grids.

Concealed underlying entity types and def descriptions when at `Contact` intelligence tier, presenting a neutral label ("Unidentified Contact") and atmospheric reconnaissance text on inspect and Info cards, while unlocking tailored labels upon classification.

Instead of silently vanishing due to vanilla visitor job-giver failures, a historical notification informs the player that the travellers arrived, found no colonists present, and departed safely.
